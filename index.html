Tip: Always ask cab drivers to turn on meter or negotiate with 'PickMe' app rates before onboarding!

Ride-Hailing & Cabs

Download PickMe and Uber as soon as you land at CMB airport. For highway runs to Ahangama, pre-fixed cabs (₹5,500) are very reliable.

Money & Cards

Your flights are covered on ICICI. In Sri Lanka, major beach cafes take Visa/Mastercard, but carry ~35,000–50,000 LKR in cash for local drivers, king coconuts, and tips.

Dialog or Mobitel SIM

Grab an eSIM or physical tourist SIM at the Bandaranaike airport arrival hall (approx $8–$12 USD for 30GB+ data).
