🌟 Key Trip Highlights

  • 🌊
    Iconic Coastal Railway: Ride alongside crashing waves from Colombo to Weligama on Sri Lanka's historic coastal track.
  • 🏄
    Weligama Surf Bay: One of Asia's finest beginner-to-intermediate surf breaks right at your doorstep at Outpost Weligama.
  • 🐋
    Mirissa Ocean Wonders: Witness migrating Blue Whales off Dondra Point and catch golden hour on Coconut Tree Hill.
  • 🏰
    UNESCO Galle Fort: Explore 400-year-old cobblestone streets, colonial lighthouses, and chic gelato cafes.
  • 🏙️
    Granbell Rooftop & Marine Drive: Unwind in the 27th-floor open-air infinity pool gazing over the Indian Ocean.

💡 Travel Advisory & Weather (Late Nov)

Ideal Transition Season: Late November marks the opening of the peak dry season on the Southern and Western coasts. Warm tropical weather (28°C–31°C) with calm ocean conditions in Weligama Bay.

• Visa (ETA): Apply online prior to travel for standard Tourist ETA or take advantage of current fee exemptions for Indian nationals at arrivals.

• Train Booking Tip: Coastal Train seats ($32/person) open 30 days prior. Aim for 1st Class Observation or 2nd Class reserved window on the right side southbound for unobstructed ocean panoramas.

• Expressway Uber: The Weligama-to-Colombo transfer via the E01 Southern Expressway is fast (~2h 15m), air-conditioned, and takes tolls into account.

DAY 3

Weligama Surf Culture & Mirissa Sunset Peaks

Monday, 23 November 2026
Beach & Adventure
08:00 – 11:30
Morning Surf Session & Beach Lounge
Weligama is celebrated as one of the world's most forgiving learn-to-surf breaks with soft sand bottoms. Take an introductory 1-on-1 surf lesson or relax on sun loungers right outside Outpost with fresh King Coconuts (Thambili).
12:30 – 15:30
Artisan Cafes & Mirissa Bay Exploration
Lunch at one of Weligama's famed bohemian cafes (Ceylon Sliders, Nomad Cafe, or Moochie's). Take a quick 10-minute tuk-tuk ride around the bay to Mirissa.
16:30 – 18:30
Climbing Coconut Tree Hill & Parrot Rock
Visit Coconut Tree Hill, a picturesque red-cliff promontory studded with towering palm trees overlooking the Indian Ocean. Capture sunset photos, then walk over to Parrot Rock for views of the curving bay.
19:30 – LATE
Candlelit Seafood Dinner on Mirissa Beach
Mirissa beach transforms at night with candlelit tables set right on the sand. Choose freshly caught jumbo prawns, red snapper, or calamari grilled to order with garlic butter and island spices.
DAY 4

Mirissa Blue Whale Safari & UNESCO Galle Fort

Tuesday, 24 November 2026
Wildlife & Heritage
06:15 – 10:30
Early Morning Whale Watching Boat Excursion
Mirissa is the premier global hub for spotting the mighty Blue Whale, the largest creature on Earth, alongside pods of spinner dolphins. Board an early morning catamaran cruise from Mirissa harbor with onboard breakfast.
13:00 – 17:30
Half-Day Tour of UNESCO Galle Dutch Fort
Take a 40-minute scenic coastal drive to Galle. Walk along the 17th-century granite ramparts built by Portuguese and Dutch colonizers. Admire the iconic Galle Lighthouse, Meeran Mosque, Dutch Reformed Church, and browse high-end boutique stores for Ceylon gems and handmade linen.
17:30 – 20:30
Sunset Rampart Walk & Heritage Dinner
Watch cliff divers and the crimson sun sink behind the sea walls from the Flag Rock bastion. Savor gourmet fusion dinner in a restored Dutch courtyard or artisan gelato on Pedlar Street before returning to Weligama.
DAY 5

Swimming with Sea Turtles & Secret Beach Sanctuary

Wednesday, 25 November 2026
Recreation
09:00 – 12:00
Snorkeling with Giant Sea Turtles at Polhena / Madiha
Head over to Polhena Beach or Turtle Bay where giant green sea turtles swim in shallow, reef-protected waters. Rent a mask and snorkel to swim peacefully alongside these gentle giants just meters from the shore.
13:00 – 16:30
Secluded Bliss at Mirissa Secret Beach & Ayurvedic Spa
Tucked behind coconut groves lies Secret Beach, a tranquil cove with crystal-clear lagoon swimming. In the afternoon, treat yourself to a traditional Sri Lankan herbal oil Ayurvedic massage.
18:00 – LATE
Final Farewell Coastal Sunset at Outpost Weligama
Celebrate your last evening in the Southern Province with cocktails at the beach deck, listening to the rhythmic surf and ambient acoustic live music.

DAY 7

Colombo Cultural Odyssey, Fine Tea & High-End Shopping

Friday, 27 November 2026
City Exploration
09:00 – 12:30
Gangaramaya Temple, Beira Lake & Independence Square
Visit Colombo's most renowned spiritual complex, Gangaramaya Temple, housing exquisite artifacts, sacred relics, and the serene Seema Malaka water pavilion designed by Geoffrey Bawa. Continue to Independence Memorial Hall and Victoria Park.
13:30 – 17:00
Shopping: Ceylon Spices, Pure Silks & One Galle Face
Indulge in retail therapy across Colombo's top spots: Barefoot for handwoven textiles and art books, Dilmah t-Lounge for premium single-origin tea tastings, Odel for fashion, and the grand One Galle Face oceanfront mall.
18:00 – 21:30
Galle Face Green Sunset & Dutch Hospital Heritage Dinner
Join locals along Galle Face Green promenade as the sky turns purple over the ocean. Try spicy Isso Wade (prawn fritters). Finish the evening with a celebratory feast in the 17th-century Old Dutch Hospital complex (Ministry of Crab, Colombo Fort Cafe, or Taprobane).

🥥 Must-Try Culinary Specialties

• Egg Hoppers (Appa): Crispy bowl-shaped coconut-rice pancakes served with spicy lunu miris and seeni sambol.
• Kottu Roti: Chopped godamba roti stir-fried on a flat iron grill with vegetables, egg, spices, and chicken/cheese.
• Mirissa Jumbo Prawns & Crab: Freshly grilled on the beach with garlic butter and island chili marinade.
• King Coconut (Thambili): Sweet golden coconut water freshly cut roadside to stay hydrated in the tropical sun.
• Pol Sambol: Fresh scraped coconut, chili, shallots, and lime juice — pairs perfectly with rice and dhal.
